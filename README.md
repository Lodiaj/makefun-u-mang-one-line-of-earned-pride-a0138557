One Line of Earned Pride — one line, a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene:

> She had practised the same three chords for two winters on a borrowed guitar, and tonight, in front of twelve people in a laundromat, she played them without stopping once.

A borrowed guitar, two winters of practice, twelve people in a laundromat — a small person doing something hard, and the long effort finally landing in one read.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` validates the line (1–240 characters and bytes, single line, not only whitespace, printable, at least 3 words, with a person marker and a verb of effort) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, then verifies the written file round-trips byte-for-byte.
